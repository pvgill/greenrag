# GreenRAG

A RAG-based web tool for assessing and optimizing the sustainability of cloud AI infrastructure.

# Overview

GreenRAG helps organizations understand the environmental impact of their AI pipelines and surface actionable optimization recommendations. Users can query their infrastructure configurations through a conversational interface and receive tailored guidance on reducing energy consumption and compute overhead.

# Research

The accompanying research examines the relationship between RAG pipeline design choices and their associated computational and energy costs, contributing to the broader literature on sustainable AI systems.
