About

The rapid growth of cloud-based AI workloads has introduced significant energy and carbon costs that are difficult for most teams to reason about holistically. GreenRAG addresses this gap by providing a conversational interface — powered by a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline — that ingests infrastructure configurations and surfaces tailored optimization recommendations grounded in current research and best practices.

Research Questions
RQ 01
Design vs. Cost
How do RAG pipeline design choices — retrieval strategy, chunk size, model selection — affect computational energy cost?
RQ 02
Optimization Gaps
What optimization strategies produce the most meaningful reductions in carbon footprint for production AI workloads?
RQ 03
Accessibility
Can a RAG-powered tool surface actionable sustainability guidance without requiring deep infrastructure expertise?
